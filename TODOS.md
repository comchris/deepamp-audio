> **Created 2026-11-12 ~20:55 Berlin at the operator's word.** The ask, verbatim: **"we should create a project-internal TODOS.md file and move all open items for versions > 0.1.0 in there"**, and when the shape of it was put back to him, his answer was **"we should do 1+3 … but we should first try to close more cards so we have to move less and can move more into changelog as done!"** — that is: this file is the home of the plugin's open work, the move happens after the cards are closed rather than before them, and what is finished belongs in `CHANGELOG.md`, not here. Copies before the change: `scratch/TODOS.md.bak_20261112_195319_pre_project_todos_split`, `scratch/AGENTS.md.bak_20261112_195319_pre_project_todos_split`; the section as it was moved is kept verbatim at `scratch/version_targets_section_20261112.txt`.

1. **What this file is.** Part one is the open-cards index: every open DEEPAMP item this tree names today, one line each, each pointing at the card that holds its history. Part two is the **version-targets section**, moved here whole from the workspace `../TODOS.md` — v0.1.0 as an open accumulation, v0.1.1, v0.1.2, v0.2.0, the filing rules, the code-freeze boundary and the pre-release items. That is what "all open items for versions > 0.1.0" names, and it is here in full, not summarised.
2. **What stays in `../TODOS.md`, and why that is not a contradiction of "one home".** The **WM cards** stay there: they are the operator's rulings in date order, several of them cross-project (the fleet, the audiobook lane, the workspace docs passes), and moving 30+ cards out of the file that dates them would lose the ordering that makes them readable. What moved is the plugin's version-target section, which was one self-contained section at the end of that file. The index in part one is the page a session reads first; a WM card is where a line's history is.
3. **How to use it.** An item is opened here with a line. A card is built and recorded in `deepamp-audio/CHANGELOG.md`, and the line here is then replaced by a pointer to that card — nothing is deleted without the dated note that says which card closed it. A version target is written only on the TODOS line, never in a `CHANGELOG.md` card (the rule of `AGENTS.md` section 1, unchanged). A card id and a quoted operator ruling live in `CHANGELOG.md` and here, not in the sources.
4. **What this file does not carry.** Fleet state, addresses, ports, model choices — read them (`endpoint_registry`, `endpoint_health`, `endpoint_models`, `queue_status`). Measured records — `docs/FACTS.md`, `docs/MODELS_TABLE.md`, `docs/MODELS_REMOTE.md`, `docs/MODEL_PROVENANCE.md`. Completed work — `CHANGELOG.md`.
